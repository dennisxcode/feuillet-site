Privacy

What never leaves your Mac.

Your school-portal username and password are stored in macOS Keychain. Portal reading runs on your Mac. Feuillet's servers do not receive the password and cannot use it.

Other devices can display a synchronized copy of what the Mac last read. The interface shows when that copy was last refreshed so old information does not look current.

Data and purpose

What Feuillet may hold, and why.

AccountName, email and account identifier, used to sign you in and keep devices synchronized.
School workCourses, timetable, assignments, exams, grades and submission state, used to build your week and show progress.
Your planningTasks, notes, completion state and study blocks, used only to provide the organizer. Personal planning is not reported to a school.
ConnectionsAuthorization tokens for services you choose to connect, used to synchronize that service until you disconnect it.
ReliabilityLimited error and synchronization records, used to diagnose failures and protect the service.
Website measurementAn allow-listed action name, page path and language. No cookies, visitor identifier, form contents or fingerprint are used. Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track disable these events.
Pilot requestsThe school and contact details submitted in the pilot form, used only to answer the request and plan the pilot.

Feuillet does not sell personal information and does not use school work or planning data for advertising.

Québec Law 25

Privacy is the default setting.

Only the student sees their own ticks, notes, captures and study plans unless they deliberately choose otherwise. A school pilot receives a written agreement before it begins, describing the information involved, its purpose, retention, access, safeguards, incident process and end-of-pilot deletion.

When consent is the basis for a feature, it must be specific and can be withdrawn. Features that are not necessary for the requested service remain off by default.

Your choices

Access, correct, export or delete.

You may ask what personal information Feuillet holds, correct inaccurate information, obtain a portable export, disconnect a service, withdraw optional consent or delete your account. In-app controls are used where available; otherwise email the address below from the email associated with your account.

Deletion requests are completed after identity is verified, subject to information that must temporarily be retained for security, legal or accounting reasons. Those exceptions will be explained rather than hidden.

Terms of use

A public beta, with honest limits.

  • Feuillet is currently free during the public beta. Material pricing changes will be announced before they take effect.
  • You are responsible for your account and for confirming important deadlines with the original school source.
  • Do not attempt to disrupt the service, access another person's data, automate abusive traffic or use Feuillet unlawfully.
  • Connected services such as Google, Todoist and a school portal have their own terms and can change or withdraw access.
  • Beta features may change and occasional interruptions are possible. Feuillet is an organization tool, not an official school record, medical service or professional advice.
  • You keep ownership of content you add. You give Feuillet only the limited permission needed to store, synchronize and display it for you.

Schools

The pilot agreement comes before the pilot.

A school agreement identifies responsibilities, approved users, the narrow evaluation question, data shared with the school, consent, support, security, incident handling, retention and deletion. Grouped pilot results do not include private student planning. Request the current agreement through the pilot form or contact address.

Accessibility

Feuillet should remain usable without motion or a pointer.

The marketing site supports keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings, reduced motion and responsive layouts. The product is being designed toward WCAG 2.2 AA, including contrast, text resizing and screen-reader labeling.

If anything prevents you from using the site or app, include the page, device and assistive technology if you are comfortable doing so. An accessible alternative or correction will be prioritized.
