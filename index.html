Before you ask

The questions everyone asks.

Can my school see what I write in it?

No. What you tick off, what you note and how you plan your week are yours. Nothing a student writes is reported to a school or a parent, and that is a rule the product is built around rather than a setting. What a school can be shown, if it ever asks, is counts with no names on them.

Do I need a Mac?

You can use the web app on any device. A Mac is recommended because portal reading happens locally in the Mac app; your other devices then show the latest synchronized copy.

Is it going to stay free?

It is free while it is being built, and there is no payment of any kind in it today. If a price ever arrives it will be for schools buying it for their students, not for the student who already installed it.

What happens to my school password?

It is encrypted into your Mac's own keychain by macOS and never leaves the machine. Our servers do not receive it and could not use it. The portal is read by your computer, which is also why reading it needs the Mac app rather than a browser tab.

My school does not use COBA.

Then the timetable and the marks will not fill themselves in yet, and everything else still works: the week, the agenda photo, Todoist, your calendar, Classroom where your school allows it. Mozaïk-Portail, Pluriportail and Omnivox are the next three, in the order schools ask for them.

What if I stop using it?

You can export everything and delete the account yourself, from inside the app. Nothing is held hostage, and disconnecting a calendar deliberately leaves the events it wrote, because those are your own copy of your work.

Who is building this?

One secondary student in Montreal, who kept quitting planners and wanted one that beat the paper agenda. Every version that has ever shipped has a public changelog: read what changed, release by release.
